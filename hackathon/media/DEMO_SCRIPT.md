# Demo script and recording plan

Target length is 2 minutes 40 seconds. Use a synthetic birthday card and capture the real app. Record the AI and checkout shots only once the deployed flow works.

| Time | Shot | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | Studio landing and card worlds | “You know exactly who you want to celebrate. Finding the words and making the card feel like them is the hard part. Ultra Cards gives you a little studio for that.” |
| 0:20–0:45 | Select Birthday and enter the rainy-hike brief | “This one's for Alex, who brought hot cocoa on a hike that turned into a downpour. I want the card to feel warm and a little funny.” |
| 0:45–1:20 | Open AI Assist, show consent, generate and apply a real suggestion | “I choose what to share with Gemini. It suggests a note and designs from our actual catalogue. I can use one design, keep my own words, or edit the draft.” |
| 1:20–2:05 | Preview and approve PayPal sandbox checkout | “The classic card is free. Signature Reveal adds a gold-foil envelope and illustrated stars. I’m using PayPal sandbox. The server verifies the capture before the upgrade appears.” |
| 2:05–2:25 | Publish and open the link in a private window | “The link opens straight to Alex's card. The recipient only needs the link.” |
| 2:25–2:40 | Architecture diagram and a final card shot | “The browser edits the card. Gemini helps with wording, PayPal handles checkout, and Firebase keeps track of the verified upgrade for that card.” |

## Recording checklist

- Check the hosted build, verify the synthetic brief and practice the real sandbox journey.
- Hide account identifiers and credentials. Capture at desktop width and capture a separate mobile still.
- Record the landing, editor, AI output, approved order and signed-out reveal as separate clips.
- Record voiceover, caption the result and keep the cut under three minutes.
- Check rights to all footage, images and audio before export. Use the actual sandbox state on screen.
- Confirm YouTube publication with the owner. The official rules require public visibility.
- Capture stills of the studio, applied suggestion and signed-out reveal. Footage and screenshots are pending provider and browser access.
